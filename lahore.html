Welcome to Lahore

Known as the cultural heart of Pakistan, Lahore is celebrated for its rich history, vibrant food scene, and stunning Mughal architecture.

Badshahi Mosque

Commissioned by Emperor Aurangzeb, this iconic red sandstone mosque stands as an architectural masterpiece facing the Lahore Fort.

Lahore Fort (Shahi Qila)

A massive citadel showcasing centuries of architectural evolution, featuring the famous Sheesh Mahal (Palace of Mirrors) and the Picture Wall.

Shalimar Gardens

A pristine Mughal garden complex laid out as a three-tier terraced paradise with beautiful fountains and marble pavilions.
